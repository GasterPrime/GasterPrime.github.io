Server engineering

The parts players never see

Fabric + Bukkit on one server

  • Runs Cobblemon mods and Bukkit plugins together on Arclight 1.21.1.
  • Patched DecentHolograms, ProtectionStones, CoreProtect and PlotSquared to work on the hybrid.
  • Bridges between both worlds, such as mod placeholders into PlaceholderAPI and economy into Vault.

Economy design

  • Two currencies with floating market prices.
  • Transfer tax and price floors to stop money printing.
  • Resource worlds that reset monthly, separate from build worlds.

Performance & operations

  • Spawn tuning such as pokemonPerChunk and despawn distance for high player counts.
  • Auto-restart loop with crash guard, plus daily restart and backup.
  • Profiling with spark, and TPS drop alerts to Discord.
